scans the apps wPurge reports installed and matches them against a catalog of official vendor IP databases; adding one feeds its ranges to wHawk. Apps wPurge no longer sees (leftovers) are handled by wPurge's own Traces tab, and there is no DNS step — domain-level blocking lives in wTower's DNS shield.

🔒 Localhost-only, zero telemetry — feeds are fetched from their publishers and served to local peers, nothing about your machine leaves it.
